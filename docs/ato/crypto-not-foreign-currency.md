# Crypto is not foreign currency (Division 775)

> **Source:**
> https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/print?DocID=TXD%2FTD201425%2FNAT%2FATO%2F00001&PiT=99991231235958
> (Taxation Determination **TD 2014/25**, "Income tax: is bitcoin a 'foreign
> currency' for the purposes of Division 775 of the *Income Tax Assessment Act
> 1997*?", consolidated version).
> **Retrieved:** 2026-08-18
> The live ATO site is authoritative; this is a convenience mirror of the
> Ruling and its date of effect — the Appendix's explanation of why bitcoin is
> not 'currency' is not reproduced.

## Ruling

> 1. Bitcoin **is not a 'foreign currency'** for the purposes of Division 775
>    of the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997* (ITAA 1997).
>
> 1A. All further legislative references in this Determination are to the ITAA
>    1997 unless otherwise indicated.

## Date of effect

> 2. This Determination applies to years of income commencing both before and
>    after its date of issue. **Schedule 2 to the *Treasury Laws Amendment
>    (2022 Measures No. 4) Act 2023* amended the definition of 'foreign
>    currency' to exclude digital currency (including bitcoin). These
>    amendments took effect for income years starting 1 July 2021.** This
>    Determination applies for all income years prior to 1 July 2021. However,
>    this Determination will not apply to taxpayers to the extent that it
>    conflicts with the terms of a settlement of a dispute agreed to before the
>    date of issue of this Determination (see paragraphs 75 and 76 of Taxation
>    Ruling TR 2006/10).

## Why it matters here

Division 775 taxes foreign-currency gains and losses as **ordinary income**,
not as capital gains, and this system does not model it (a documented Known
limitation for foreign-currency *cash* balances). A crypto asset — including a
stablecoin held as an investment — is outside Division 775 both under this
Determination and, from the 2021–22 income year, under the statutory
definition. So the deferral never reaches a crypto holding: it is a CGT asset
like any other crypto (`crypto-cgt.md`), which is how it is treated here.
